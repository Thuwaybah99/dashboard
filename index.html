01 / CONTEXT

The business story

BazarNova's retail extract contains sales observations from 2017. The objective is to understand what drives recorded revenue, compare store and product performance, and identify where promotional patterns warrant follow-up.

Rather than treating sales as one number, this analysis connects time, product hierarchy, promotions and store location to examine where sales are concentrated and which patterns deserve business attention.

Coverage is sparse: records appear on only 18 dates (the first two days of each month, April–December). The results describe this extract, not complete monthly sales or true seasonality.

02 / FRAMING

Questions behind the analysis

Six different questions. Six purpose-built charts. No duplicated visual storytelling.

Q1

When and why did recorded revenue spike?

Separate unusual promotional activity from the underlying time pattern.

Q2

Which products generate the most revenue?

Identify concentration and dependencies on individual products.

Q3

What distinguishes store performance?

Compare revenue with demand volume rather than rankings alone.

Q4

What can we learn from promotions?

Identify standout promotion records without claiming incremental uplift.

Q5

Does demand align with revenue?

Distinguish high-unit products from high-value products.

Q6

Can stock-related records support decisions?

Assess data completeness before interpreting zero sales or availability.

06 / SYNTHESIS

What the evidence suggests

Evidence-based takeaways from the supplied sales extract; percentages use recorded revenue as denominator.

01PRODUCT RISK

One product represents a third of recorded value.

Portable Heater accounts for about 33.5% of total recorded revenue. Examine reliance on this product and whether it is repeatable.

02REVENUE SPIKE

December is dominated by an exceptional event.

About 66.5% of observed revenue falls in December, but only two days were sampled. Avoid annual seasonality claims.

03PROMOTIONS

Eight PR16 observations account for 30.7% of value.

They all involve Portable Heater on December 1; the pattern requires validation and cannot establish promotional causality.

04STORES

Revenue and units paint different store profiles.

Electro World generates slightly higher revenue with fewer recorded units than DIGI, potentially reflecting different product mixes.

!

How far can these findings go?

This extract contains 16,383 sales observations across just 18 dates in April–December 2017, and only six stores. The revenue currency is unspecified. Revenue is not profit; sales records cannot prove uplift, inventory stockouts, or full-month demand trends.
